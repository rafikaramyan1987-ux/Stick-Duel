A turn-based physics duel where direct hits barely hurt: use the world instead! Cut ropes to drop rocks and chandeliers, burn roofs, swing log rams, freeze lakes, set off barrels and tip your rival into lava. 6 worlds, 48 levels + 6 bosses, 5 kinds of arrows, slow-motion replays, 2 players on one device, Endless mode and hats to collect.
