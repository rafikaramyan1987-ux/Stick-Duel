Drag back anywhere and release to shoot (mouse or touch): the dotted line shows the start of the flight, the meter shows the power. Keyboard: arrow keys aim and set power, Space shoots, 1-5 pick the ammo, P pauses. You and your rival take turns. Head 35, body 20, arms and legs 10 damage. Break the cover, or shoot ropes, barrels and stoppers: traps hit for up to 80. Fire burns wood, heavy bolts crack stone, ice freezes water, bombs blast. Stars: win, keep half your HP, win with a trap.
