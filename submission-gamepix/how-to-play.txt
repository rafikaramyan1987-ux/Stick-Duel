Drag back anywhere and release to shoot (mouse or touch): the dotted line shows the start of the flight, the meter shows the power. Keyboard: arrow keys aim and set power, Space shoots, 1-5 pick the ammo, P pauses. Direct hits do little damage: shoot ropes, roofs, barrels and supports so falling and flying things hit your rival. Fire arrows burn wood and light barrels, heavy bolts crack stone, ice arrows freeze water, bombs blast. Win within par shots and with a trick shot for 3 stars.
