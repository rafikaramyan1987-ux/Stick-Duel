Topple Duel is a turn-based physics duel where direct hits barely hurt: use the world instead! Cut ropes to drop rocks and chandeliers, burn wooden roofs, swing log rams, freeze lakes into bridges, set off barrels and tip your rival into lava. Six worlds, 48 levels plus 6 boss arenas, five kinds of arrows, slow-motion replays of your best chain reactions, a 2-player mode on one device, Endless mode and hats to collect. Drag back, release, topple!
