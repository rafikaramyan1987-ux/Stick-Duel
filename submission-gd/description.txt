Topple Duel is a turn-based archery duel with real physics. Take turns with your rival: a headshot does 35, the body 20, arms and legs 10. Hide behind cover that wears down under arrows, then spring the traps: cut a rope to drop a rock, light a barrel, free a boulder, knock your rival into the lake. Six worlds, 48 levels and 6 bosses, five kinds of arrows, wind, swinging targets, a 2-player mode on one device, Endless mode and hats to collect.
